# Expectant mothers

## Travelling while pregnant

Travel is allowed up to the 32nd week of pregnancy if there are no complications. If there is a gap between the departure date and the booking date, a medical certificate from an obstetrician is required, issued within 72 hours of departure, affirming the pregnancy has no complications.

## Travelling beyond the 32nd week of pregnancy

- Up to the 35th week, if a normal delivery is anticipated, with a medical certificate confirming fitness to travel and no complications.
- Beyond the 35th week, travel is not allowed, for safety reasons.

## Exception

Travel is not allowed after the 32nd week in case of multiple pregnancies (twins, triplets, and so on) or a history of complicated pregnancy or delivery, such as a previous miscarriage.

## In the event of an emergency

Fill out the MEDIF form and seek permission from the Executive Director of Medical Services, via customer service.

## At the airport and in the air

Provisions include wheelchair assistance, medical rooms and pharmacies at most airports, flexible dining options, and extendable seat belts provided by the in-flight crew.

# Infants

## Who is an infant and what is the minimum age of travel?

Children over seven days but under two years on the travel date are considered infants. The minimum age of travel is seven days, with an exception for medical emergencies, which require a paediatrician's certificate and medical department clearance.

## Documents

Valid ID proof is required to verify the infant's age: a birth certificate, a vaccination certificate, or a passport (mandatory for international travel). Without proof, an adult fare is charged, subject to seat availability.

## Guidelines for travelling with more than one infant

Each baby must be accompanied by an adult and seated on the adult's lap; no extra seats can be booked for babies.

## Guidelines for an infant travelling with an authorised guardian

Requires a signed consent letter from the legal parent or guardian, ID proof confirming parentage, and the guardian's own valid ID proof. Additional documents may apply for travel outside the country of residence.

## At the airport and in the air

- Nursing rooms are available at most airports.
- Strollers are not allowed on the flight, but a free bassinet can be requested, subject to availability.
- Infant check-in baggage allowance: 10 kg/22 lb in Economy, 12 kg/26 lb in Premium Economy, and 15 kg/33 lb in Business and First Class.
- Carry-on baggage allowed for infants: food, feeding bottles, and a carry-on tote or bag for these items.
- Priority check-in and boarding are available on request.
