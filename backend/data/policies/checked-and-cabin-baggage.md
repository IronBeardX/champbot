# Checked baggage
<!-- source_url: https://www.airindia.com/in/en/travel-information/baggage-guidelines/checked-baggage-allowance.html -->

Discover what you need to know about checked baggage allowances for domestic and international Air India flights.

## Domestic flights

| Cabin class | Brand | Baggage |
|---|---|---|
| Economy | Value | 15 kg/33 lb |
| Economy | Classic | 20 kg/44 lb |
| Economy | Flex | 25 kg/55 lb |
| Premium Economy | Value | 15 kg/33 lb |
| Premium Economy | Flex | 25 kg/55 lb |
| Business | Value | 30 kg/66 lb |
| Business | Flex | 40 kg/88 lb |
| First | First | 40 kg/88 lb |

Points to remember:

- The above allowances apply only to Air India-operated flights, not to Air India Express or codeshare flights.
- Passengers flying Air India domestic and international sectors on the same ticket get the international sector's allowance.
- Passengers on separate domestic and connecting-international tickets can use the Free Baggage Allowance (FBA) if the connection is within 24 hours and both flights are operated by Air India.
- Star Alliance Gold members can carry an additional 20 kg/44 lb in economy class.
- Maharaja Club members get additional baggage.
- Infants are entitled to one collapsible stroller, carrycot or infant car seat.
- The maximum weight for a single piece of baggage is 32 kg/70 lb across the entire Air India network.
- Passengers with an FBA under 25 kg get 1 piece of check-in baggage; those with 25 kg or more get 2 pieces. Charges may apply beyond the allowed number of pieces.

### Rules for baggage dimensions

| Cabin class | Dimension allowance |
|---|---|
| Economy/Premium Economy | Combined dimensions (L+B+H) of both pieces must not exceed 272 cm/107 in, and each piece must not exceed 158 cm/62 in. |
| First/Business | Combined dimensions (L+B+H) of each piece must not exceed 158 cm/62 in. |

## International flights

Allowance varies by region and fare brand. Selected examples:

| Route | Economy Value | Economy Classic/Flex | Premium Economy | Business | First |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| India-Sri Lanka | 30 kg | 30-40 kg | 30-40 kg | 40-45 kg | 45 kg |
| India-Nepal/Maldives | 20 kg | 20-30 kg | 20-30 kg | 35-40 kg | 40 kg |
| India-Gulf/Middle East | 25 kg | 30-35 kg | 25-35 kg | 40-45 kg | 45 kg |
| India-Europe | 1 piece of 23 kg | 2 pieces of 23 kg | 2 pieces of 23 kg | 2 pieces of 32 kg | 2 pieces of 32 kg |
| India-United States/Canada | 1 piece of 23 kg | 2 pieces of 23 kg | 2 pieces of 23 kg | 2 pieces of 32 kg | 2 pieces of 32 kg |

### Piece and weight concept

| Cabin class | Maximum sum of dimensions | Maximum weight per piece |
|---|---|---|
| First/Business | 158 cm/62 in | 32 kg/70 lb |
| Economy/Premium Economy | 271 cm/107 in (both pieces combined) | 23 kg/50 lb |

# Cabin baggage
<!-- source_url: https://www.airindia.com/in/en/travel-information/baggage-guidelines/cabin-baggage.html -->

Cabin baggage allowances apply to adult, child and infant passengers travelling on a purchased seat, subject to the size and weight limits below. Passengers may also carry one small personal item, such as a handbag or a bag for electronic devices, that fits under the seat in front of them.

## Cabin baggage size and weight limits

| Class of travel | Maximum weight | Size limitations |
|---|---|---|
| Premium Economy and Economy | 7 kg/15 lb | Height 55 cm/21 in, length 40 cm/15 in, width 20 cm/7 in; total dimensions must not exceed 115 cm/45 in |
| First and Business Class | 10 kg/22 lb | Same size limits as above |

## Notes on cabin baggage

- Passengers may carry only one piece of hand baggage, in compliance with Indian security regulations (BCAS "One Hand Baggage Rule", Circular 6/2000, dated 24 Feb 2000). This applies to every class of travel, regardless of Maharaja Club or Star Alliance frequent flyer status, for both international and domestic flights.
- A cabin bag weighing up to 12 kg can be carried only after paying the applicable excess hand baggage charges for weight above the free allowance: USD 75 on international flights, INR 3000 on domestic flights.
- A cabin bag weighing more than 12 kg must be checked in, with the applicable excess baggage charges.
