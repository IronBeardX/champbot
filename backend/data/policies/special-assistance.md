# Medical Clearance and Paperwork

**Who needs medical clearance to fly?**

Most passengers with medical conditions can fly by submitting a Medical Information Form (MEDIF). Medical clearance is required when: fitness to travel is in doubt due to recent illness, hospitalisation, injury or surgery; the passenger has a pre-existing medical condition; special services are needed, such as oxygen or medical equipment; or the passenger is flying for medical reasons or treatment. Contact the nearest Air India office; boarding is only possible once the completed MEDIF has been received and evaluated.

# Wheelchairs

**Who qualifies for wheelchair assistance? Is there an additional charge?**

Passengers with reduced mobility or certified medical conditions can request wheelchair assistance. Air India's medical officers review the submitted medical information to approve a wheelchair and an assistant. Wheelchair assistance is free. It should be requested in advance, either during booking or at least 48 hours before scheduled departure.

# Bringing Medical Supplies On Board

**Do I need to declare a battery-powered mobility aid or wheelchair?**

Fill out the Battery Powered Wheelchair Information Form and submit it under the Airport Services - Health and Medical Assistance tab at least 48 hours before departure. One mobility aid is carried free in addition to the baggage allowance; mobility aids over 30 kg/66 lb need special permission.

# Inflight Health & Wellness

**I have dietary restrictions. Can I order special meals?**

Special meals available include a diabetic meal, a gluten-free meal, a fruit platter meal, and a vegan meal. All dietary meals are vegetarian. Order at least 24 hours before departure via the website.
