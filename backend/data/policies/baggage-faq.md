# Baggage Allowance

**What is the total number of bags I can check in for my flight?**

The checked baggage allowance for a flight varies by travel class, fare family, route and Maharaja Club member status. Determine the exact allowance with the Booking Reference Number (PNR) and last name in the baggage calculator, or with the baggage allowance option in AI.g or the mobile app. Be mindful of weight and size limits to avoid extra charges.

# From Check-in to Claims

**How do I check my baggage if I have checked in for the flight online?**

1. Review the baggage guidelines for the allowed weight, size and number of bags for the travel class and destination.
2. Arrive at the airport with enough time before departure (the check-in window is usually available from 48 hours up to 60 minutes before departure).
3. Head to the designated baggage drop counter, separate from the regular check-in counters, for passengers who checked in online but still need to drop baggage.
4. Present the e-ticket and an ID such as a passport or driver's licence.
5. Airline staff weigh the bags and tag them with destination labels.
6. Collect the boarding pass at this stage if it was not already issued during online check-in.

# Baggage on Connecting Flights

**Is my checked baggage transferred to the final destination on a connecting flight, or do I need to check in again during transit?**

Baggage transfer on a connecting flight depends on the route (international-to-domestic or domestic-to-international with a layover) and the type of booking: a single ticket flying Air India or partner airlines throughout, or separate tickets with different airlines for different segments.

# Baggage Policy for Infants/Children

**What is the baggage allowance for unaccompanied minors and children?**

Unaccompanied minors and children are allowed to check in the same amount of baggage as any adult passenger on that flight.

# Excess Baggage

**What is the fee for booking excess baggage on a flight?**

The cost varies by route, travel class and the weight of the additional baggage, using a tiered pricing structure where charges increase with weight; charges differ for domestic and international flights. The official Excess Baggage page has the exact costs. Pre-booking excess baggage before the flight is usually more cost-effective than paying at the airport.

# Issues with Baggage

**How do I report damage to my baggage during a flight?**

1. Inspect the baggage immediately on arrival and report visible damage to airline staff at the baggage claim area before leaving the airport.
2. File a Property Irregularity Report (PIR), the official document describing the baggage's condition and the reported damage; keep a copy.
3. Provide flight details, baggage tags, contact information, a description of the damage, and photographs if possible.
4. Track the baggage with the WorldTracer baggage tracking system after filing the PIR.

Report damage as soon as possible to improve the chances of a successful claim.

# Limits and Restrictions

**Can I carry alcohol on Air India?**

On international flights, each passenger may carry a maximum of 5 litres of alcohol in checked baggage, up to 70% alcohol by volume, in its original retail packaging, sealed, with receipts kept handy.

# Special Items

**What is the baggage policy for passengers with disabilities or medical conditions?**

Essential medical equipment, such as wheelchairs, is carried free of charge and does not count towards the regular baggage allowance. Passengers may carry essential medication in cabin baggage regardless of the usual liquid or gel restrictions, provided they have the prescribed documents.
