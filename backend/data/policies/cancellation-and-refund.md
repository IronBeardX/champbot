# Refund

**How can I request a refund for my booking?**

Via the website (manage booking section, then the refund option) or the mobile app (the 'My Trips' option, on Android and iOS).

# Cancellations

**Can I cancel my booking and request a refund using the app?**

Yes, using the app, available for Android and iOS.

# Rescheduling

**Do I need to pay additional charges when rescheduling my flight?**

Charges are calculated based on the rescheduling time window, the fare type, and the route of the existing and new booking. If the flight was cancelled by Air India and self-service re-accommodation is used to reschedule, no additional charges apply, since it is a free service.

# Self-service re-accommodation

**What is self-service re-accommodation and how can I use this service?**

It lets a passenger rebook a flight Air India had to cancel, without extra charges.

1. Go to the self-service re-accommodation page, or the link emailed after the cancellation.
2. Enter the Booking Reference (PNR) and last name.
3. Choose from up to 20 alternative flights spread over 75 hours around the original departure time; flights with no layovers are listed first.
4. Review and confirm the new itinerary.
5. Receive a confirmation email with the receipt and the new reservation details.

# Payment

**What are the various payment methods available when booking a flight with Air India?**

Credit cards (American Express, Visa, Mastercard, Diners Club, Discover, JCB), internet banking, RuPay cards, e-wallets, EMI options, and UPI.
