# Delayed baggage

If baggage does not arrive within the specified period, contact Air India personnel in the arrival hall to report the issue and file a Property Irregularity Report (PIR); a copy is provided. A Mishandled Baggage Statement can be generated online. Essential expenses incurred while waiting for delayed baggage may be reimbursed, subject to terms and conditions. Delivery timelines may vary with local customs regulations at the destination airport. Status can be checked through the WorldTracer baggage tracking system.

## Australia baggage service centre

A ground-handling agent provides a baggage service call centre for Sydney and Melbourne airports. Contact the local Air India office for lost baggage claims; a claim is filed after verification, with a reference number to use with the call centre, available 24x7 on the toll-free number 1800 452 308 or by email at baggageservices@dnata.com.

# Lost baggage

Baggage is categorised as lost if it is untraceable for 21 days from the date the PIR was filed. Claims must be substantiated with an itemised list of contents, a description, the item's age, and proof of purchase or ownership.

# Damaged baggage

Normal wear and tear, such as minor cuts, scratches, scuffs, stains, dents, or soiling, is not covered. Air India is not responsible for damage to overpacked or oversized bags, bags with fragile or perishable items, or baggage with manufacturing defects. Valuables such as jewellery, cash, negotiable papers, and electronics should not be carried in checked baggage; Air India is not liable for their loss, damage, or delay, and travel insurance is recommended. Report damaged or pilfered baggage to Air India personnel at the arrival hall.

# Limitation of Liability

Air India's liability for delayed, lost, or damaged baggage is subject to applicable laws and international conventions, and is limited accordingly. The Air India Conditions of Carriage have the detailed terms.
