# Online check-in

**What is online check-in?**

A convenient, time-saving service to check in through a computer or mobile device with internet connectivity, via the website, the app, or WhatsApp (AI.g, for domestic travel). To check in: open the web check-in page or the app, enter the Booking Reference and the passenger's last name, then follow the on-screen instructions to confirm details and select seats and meals (an unselected seat is auto-assigned). The boarding pass is generated at the end and can be viewed or saved on the device.

Online check-in is available within 48 hours up to 60 minutes before the scheduled departure time. For international flights departing Delhi airport, the check-in counter opens 75 minutes before scheduled departure.

# Airport check-in

**How soon should I arrive at the airport for my flight?**

It is recommended to reach the airport at least 3 hours before the scheduled departure time.

# Boarding pass

**Will I be issued a boarding pass after checking in online?**

Domestic travellers get a digital boarding pass (mobile or printable PDF) when checking in online. Passengers travelling internationally, or domestic followed by an international flight, may instead receive a confirmation slip, to be exchanged for a boarding pass at the airport counter after verification.

# Other issues

**Can I undo check-in if I decide not to travel?**

Yes. Visit the Air India counter at the origin airport, or call the nearest Air India centre for assistance.
